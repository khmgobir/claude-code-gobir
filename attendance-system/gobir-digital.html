Questions

Good to know

Do I need any experience?

Not at all. Sessions are built for complete beginners and improvers alike. We start from wherever you are and keep it simple.

How do online sessions work?

You'll get a Google Meet or Zoom link after your booking is confirmed. All you need is a phone or laptop and internet.

How do I pay?

After you book, you'll receive a confirmation with payment details (bank transfer). Payment confirms your slot.

Can I bring friends or my team?

Yes — up to 5 people can join one session. Just choose the number of people when booking (₦5,000 each).

What if I need to reschedule?

No problem. Reach out on WhatsApp at least a few hours before your slot and we'll find a new time that works.

Privacy & data protection

Your data is safe with us

We collect only what's needed to arrange your session, and we protect it. Here's exactly how.

What we collect
Only your name, phone/WhatsApp, email, chosen topic, mode, date/time, number of people, and any note you add.
Why

Your rights

Only as long as needed for your sessions and basic records; removed on request.
